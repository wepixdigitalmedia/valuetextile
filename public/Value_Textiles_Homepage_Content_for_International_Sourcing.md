## What changed in this version

- **Fabric-led became fabric + garments + full package.** The first draft followed the UI reference too closely, so the copy read like a fabric catalogue. Garments now have the same weight as fabrics, and a "full package" route (fabric and garment in one order) is added.
- **New sections:** Why Tirupur (the international position), Ways to work with us, and sourcing support.
- **Removed fabric-marketplace patterns:** search-first header, price per metre, "Made to Order / Dyed / Dyeable" categories and the fabric-only carousel.
- **Reference sites are now used only for what they are good at.** The UI reference gives layout and behaviour only. WePix gives a checklist of what overseas buyers expect to see, never its wording. Your current site gives the facts.
- **Items from the first draft that your current site does not confirm are now marked [CONFIRM]:** T-shirts and polos, kids and baby wear, the fabric constructions (single jersey, rib, interlock, fleece, pique) and "low minimum orders".

## Positioning and writing rules

**Who we are:** a knitwear manufacturer and exporter in Tirupur, India. We make fabrics **and** garments in our own units, so an overseas brand deals with one team, one quality standard and one export desk, from yarn to shipment.

**Positioning line:** Knitted fabrics and garments for international brands, made from yarn to finished garment under one roof in Tirupur, India.

**Three ways a buyer can start (use these everywhere):**

1. **Fabric:** knitted fabric developed and made to your specification.
2. **Garments:** finished garments made to your design, tech pack or reference sample.
3. **Full package:** fabric and garment developed, made, inspected and shipped as one order.

**Content balance rule (the one that stops the fabric-only drift).** Every section that talks about fabric must also cover garments or the full package, and the other way round. At most one section on the homepage may be fabric-only (the fabric spec cards). If a section reads as a fabric catalogue, rewrite it.

**What each source is for**

| Source | Use it for | Never use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UI reference site | Layout, spacing, component shapes, scroll and motion behaviour | Words, headings, claims, statistics, product types, categories, images, nav labels |
| WePix Sourcing | A checklist of topics overseas buyers expect (low MOQ, sampling, process, industries, certificates, quote form) | Its wording, claims, certificates, buyer segments copied one for one |
| valuetextiles.net (current site) | Facts: divisions, fabrics, numbers, address, phone | Weak copy ("100% customer satisfaction", "mark of excellence") and broken images |

**Tone:** plain, specific and buyer-first. Say what the buyer gets (order size, samples, lead time, shipping), not what the factory owns.

**Avoid:** excellence, world-class, cutting-edge, seamless, solutions, passionate, elevate, unlock, one-stop, leading, premium, AI-powered, "your trusted partner", "100% customer satisfaction". Do not use "ethical" or "sustainable" unless a named certificate backs it.

**Spelling:** your address and domain use "Tirupur", and many buyers also search "Tiruppur". Use **Tirupur** in headings, titles and meta text, and write "Tirupur (also spelled Tiruppur)" once in the Why Tirupur section. Write "Value Textiles" everywhere (not "Value Textile").
