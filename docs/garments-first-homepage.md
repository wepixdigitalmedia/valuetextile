## Positioning and rules

Value Textiles is now a garment manufacturer and exporter in Tirupur that makes its own fabric. Garments are the product. Fabric and yarn are not sold as a product line; they are done on request.

**Positioning line:** Knitwear garments for international brands, made in Tirupur with our own yarn, fabric and dyeing behind every order.

**Garments we lead with:** T-shirts and polos; kids and baby wear; men's and women's knitwear; activewear and loungewear; and other styles to buyer requirements, at export quality and in scalable volumes. \[CONFIRM the exact styles and sizes in each category\]

**Two ways to order garments**

1. **Full package:** we make the garment with our own fabric. One price, one delivery date.
2. **Cut and make:** we stitch the garment from fabric you supply.

**Fabric and yarn rule.** Say it once, plainly: "Need fabric or yarn only? We take these on request." It appears in the FAQ, on the Capabilities page and as one option in the quote form. There are no fabric cards, fabric carousel, fabric spec lines or fabric-roll hero images.

**Content rule.** Every section talks about garments. Fabric appears only as proof of control: our own fabric, our own dyeing, our own lab.

**Tone:** plain, specific and buyer-first. Say what the buyer gets (order size, samples, lead time, shipping).

**Avoid:** excellence, world-class, cutting-edge, seamless, solutions, passionate, elevate, unlock, one-stop, leading, premium, AI-powered, "your trusted partner", "100% customer satisfaction". Do not use "ethical" or "sustainable" unless a named certificate backs it.

**Spelling:** use Tirupur in headings, titles and meta text, and mention "Tiruppur" once. Write "Value Textiles" everywhere.
