Theme: not just a manufacturer. A vertically integrated production platform that controls every stage from knit to finished, certified, packed product. One partner, full accountability.

## What competitors do (homepages read Nov 2026)
- Eastman, KPR, Esquel, Shahi all lead with scale or tenure ("40 years", "150 million garments", "1,00,000+ employees") and say "vertically integrated" as a label.
- None of them names one accountable contact, a lead time, or how bought-in inputs are controlled. Sustainability and awards sit at the bottom.
- We do not compete on volume. We compete on a named chain, proof at each stage, and one owner of the result.

- Yarn is bought in, tested in-house. Never say spinning or "yarn to garment". Say "from tested yarn" or "from knit".
- Every claim has proof beside it. `[CONFIRM]` = only the owner can supply it; do not publish until replaced.
