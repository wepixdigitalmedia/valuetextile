# Site plan: pages, flow and conversion (decided Nov 27, 2026)

Goal: every page moves a buyer one step toward "Get a quote". Homepage is done (v1). This plan covers the other pages.

## Competitor read (homepages and navigation only)
Sites read: Eastman Exports, KPR Mill, Shahi Exports, Esquel, Gokaldas Exports. Crystal Group and Pratibha did not load.
- All 5 have an About page. 4 of 5 have Sustainability and Contact. Only KPR has a page per production stage, a Quality page and a company-profile PDF.
- None has a quote form, sample request, factory-visit booking, how-to-order page or clients page.
- Conclusion: they are corporate brochure sites. They are a floor, not a benchmark for conversion.

## Decisions made
- Products (fabric and yarn): fold into Capabilities. Remove /products. Fabric-only stays "on request" as a section on the Fabric division page.
- Merge: Infrastructure into Capabilities. Industries into Who we work with. Resources into Blog. Old addresses redirect.
- Add: How to order, Thank-you page, Privacy and Terms, Company profile download.
- Do not add: Careers, News, Investors.

## Page template
1. Headline that states the outcome for the buyer.
2. Proof you can check: numbers, certificates, real photos.
3. Three buyer doubts answered in plain words.
4. One "Get a quote" block, with the page context pre-selected.
5. Short FAQ where it helps.

## Work order
1. Quote path: Contact page as the quote page, Thank-you page, Privacy and Terms.
2. Garments hub and 4 category pages.
3. Capabilities hub and 4 division pages, with Infrastructure and Products folded in.
4. Quality and Certificates.
5. About and Who we work with.
6. How to order, Company profile download.
7. Redirects, sitemap, robots, 404, metadata.

## Needed from the owner

Full list: MOQ, sample and bulk lead times, payment terms, ports, lab test list, AQL level, traceability by lot, buyer logo permissions, the company profile PDF (or approval for me to draft one), Zero Liquid Discharge status, whether woven garments are made, legal company name and registration details for Privacy and Terms.
