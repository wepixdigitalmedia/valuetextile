## 6. Effluent treatment and water

- 1,000 m³/day biological pre-treatment.
- 626 m³/day reverse osmosis plant, fully automated, from SIMEM, Italy.
- Colour removal by ozone.
- 125 m³/day multi-effect evaporation system and 50 m³/day forced circulation unit.
- Agitated thin-film drier for the final sludge.
- Stated result: water and salt are recycled and the plant meets **zero liquid discharge (ZLD)** under government norms.

This is a strong point for European and US buyers. Before you publish "zero liquid discharge" as a claim, get the pollution control board consent or ZLD compliance letter and its date, and show that document.

## 8. Fabric inspection

- The **4-point system** is the standard. A **10-point system** is also done when a customer asks.

**Rolls checked per lot**

| Rolls in the lot | Rolls to be checked |
| --- | --- |
| 100 and above | Per the square root system |
| 50 to 99 | Minimum 10 |
| 25 to 49 | Minimum 7 |
| 11 to 24 | Minimum 5 |
| 1 to 10 | All rolls |

**Points allowed per 100 m** [CONFIRM unit: the industry norm is usually per 100 square yards or per 100 square metres]

| Points | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 30 | Pass |
| 31 to 40 | Needs approval from the head of department |
| 41 and above | Rejected |

**Roll sticker colours**

| Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Red | Not OK |
| Orange | Conditionally approved (cut these rolls first) |
| Yellow | Accepted (cut after orange) |
| Green | Passed (cut after yellow) |

A buyer-facing line from this: "Every lot is inspected on the 4-point system, with 10-point inspection on request, and every roll carries a colour-coded status sticker." The sticker system is an unusual, concrete detail worth showing with a photo.
