## Legal pages: owner and legal adviser to confirm
Privacy and Terms are drafted in plain language but must be read by the owner, ideally a legal adviser, before launch.
- Reply time: the site promises a reply within 24 working hours. Confirm the team can keep that.
- Confidentiality: Terms say designs and tech packs are treated as confidential and used only for the enquiry or order. Confirm the owner is happy to promise this.
- Retention: Privacy says enquiries are kept "as long as needed", with no fixed period. Add a number of months if the owner prefers.
- Governing law: Terms say the laws of India. Add a court location only on legal advice.
- Cookies and analytics: the site currently uses none. When tracking is added later, Privacy must be updated first.
- Data location: enquiries are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Confirm the owner is comfortable with this.
